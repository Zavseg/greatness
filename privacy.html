Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GREATNESS FAMILY handles information when you use our website and sign in to your account.

Information we collect

When you sign in, our authentication provider may provide basic account information such as your email address, display name, profile image, and a unique account identifier. We use this information only to authenticate you, maintain your account, and control access to website features.

Authentication and service providers

Authentication is provided through Supabase and may use third-party identity providers such as Google or Discord. These providers process information according to their own privacy policies. We do not receive or store your Google or Discord password.

How we use information

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To identify your account and provide appropriate website access.
  • To operate, maintain, and protect GREATNESS FAMILY services.

Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers used to operate the website, including authentication and hosting providers, only as necessary to provide those services.

Data retention and deletion

Account information is retained while it is needed to provide access to the website. If you want your GREATNESS FAMILY account information deleted, contact us using the support email shown on the Google sign-in consent screen.

Security

We use reasonable technical measures and trusted service providers to protect account information. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the website or its services change. The latest version will always be published on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions or account deletion requests, please use the support contact provided on the GREATNESS Google sign-in consent screen.
